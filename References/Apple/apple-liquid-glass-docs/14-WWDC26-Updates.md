# WWDC 2026 Liquid Glass Updates

At WWDC 2026, Apple introduced refinements to Liquid Glass:

## macOS Golden Gate / iOS 27 Updates
- **Transparency slider**: Added user-facing control for Liquid Glass transparency
- **Refined materials**: Further optimization of Liquid Glass rendering performance
- **Modernize your AppKit app**: WWDC 2026 session focused on updating AppKit apps for Liquid Glass

## Key WWDC 2026 Session
- "Modernize your AppKit app" - How to update existing AppKit applications for Liquid Glass and the new macOS design

## macOS Tahoe 26 (Current)
Liquid Glass was first introduced on macOS Tahoe 26, with:
- Redesigned windows with rounded corners
- Floating sidebars and toolbars
- Glass navigation elements
- Updated controls (sliders, toggles, buttons)
- Background extension effects

## macOS 27 Golden Gate (Announced WWDC 2026)
- Further Liquid Glass refinements
- Intel Mac dropped, Apple Silicon only
- Enhanced transparency controls
